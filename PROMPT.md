# The prompt

This is the exact prompt sent to Claude Opus 5.5 on claude.ai. It was sent once, with no later feature-change requests.

The prompt was drafted with Grok, following the structure of [@vib3coded's tennis prompt](https://x.com/vib3coded/status/2117018336605172040): build a core plus a CONTRACT.md first, then build the modules in parallel with separate agents.

```text
Build a real-time 3D pixel-art diorama of a busy neighbourhood gym, "Ironvale Fitness Club", as ONE self-contained HTML page: three.js r128 from cdnjs (no other external assets, no localStorage/eval/alert). Publish it as an artifact and also give me a standalone offline HTML file.

LOOK: a two-storey gym building on a small floating island; orthographic isometric camera at 31 degrees, quarter turns (Q/E), wheel zoom, drag to orbit/pan, follow camera; render to a low-res target with a "Pixel size" slider 1-6 (default 2), depth outlines, a fixed 90-colour palette (OKLab snap) and Bayer dither; hue-shifted ramps (shadows drift to violet, lights to warm yellow); long hard shadows; the walls and roof facing the camera lift away (doll's-house cutaway). Zones: free-weight area with racks, benches and a dumbbell wall; squat racks and lifting platforms; machine row (cable stack, leg press, lat pulldown); cardio line (treadmills, bikes, rowers) by big windows; stretching/yoga room with mats; boxing corner with heavy bags; front desk with turnstiles, smoothie bar, locker rooms (doors only, no interiors), rooftop with a small running track. Mirrors, rubber floor tiles, chalk dust, TV screens, posters. Original/fictional names and brands only. English UI.

SIMULATION: 20-40 members with varied bodies, outfits and hair, plus 2 personal trainers and 1 front-desk staff. Each member arrives, checks in, changes, follows a workout plan (strength / cardio / mixed / class), queues for busy equipment, rests between sets, wipes equipment, drinks water, chats, checks a phone, and leaves. Real exercise animations with correct form: squat, bench press, deadlift, overhead press, pull-up, dumbbell curl, rowing machine, treadmill run, cycling, plank, stretching, bag work. Plates are loaded and unloaded on bars; weights rise and fall with real timing (concentric ~1 s, eccentric ~2 s, rest 60-180 s). Group classes run on a schedule in the studio with an instructor. Time of day: Morning / Lunch / Evening peak / Late night, with crowd size following realistic peaks (busiest 6-8 pm). A day clock runs and the gym opens at 6:00 and closes at 23:00.

PAGE: side control panel (stacked below on phones): time of day and speed, pause, crowd size, open/close the gym, start a class; Member inspector (click a member: name, today's plan, current set and reps, weight, heart-rate zone); "Follow" camera on a member; camera modes (whole club / zone close-up / follow) and view controls; Stats centre (current occupancy, busiest equipment, average wait, total kg lifted today); an activity feed ("Mina hit a new squat PR: 80 kg"). Synthesized WebAudio sound (plates clanking, treadmill hum, dropped dumbbells, gym music, chatter, class instructor cues), off until the user enables it.

ENGINEERING: write the core (render pipeline, layout/plan, NAV grid, lighting) and a detailed CONTRACT.md (world conventions, module files, global names, BUS events, data model), then build the modules in parallel with separate agents in their own workspaces: building+zones, equipment meshes, people rig and actors, exercise kinematics, member AI and scheduling (generator-coroutine scheduler with watchdogs), equipment queueing, classes, crowd+fx, audio, UI. Enforce unique global names (dupcheck), then integrate, run a full day at every time setting, check four views, phone layout, performance (60 fps with 40 members). Reply to me in short Korean, don't paste the artifact URL unless asked.
```
